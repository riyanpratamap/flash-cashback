## Maker's assumptions

- Redemption answers only dismissTo('/') + acknowledge until P5.5: acceptable stopgap; carry into P5.5 (F4).
- Navigation tested only against the router mock: acceptable; `dismissTo` confirmed from source; walkthrough covers it.
